## Key traps encoded
Controller/NAND/DRAM unprinted; "3D NAND" hides TLC/QLC; DRAM-less/HMB; up-to speeds measured in SLC cache and differing by capacity;
post-cache write speed and SLC cache size never in datasheets; TBW per capacity; M.2 SATA vs NVMe key; 2230/2280 siblings; heatsink SKUs (PS5);
portable USB bridge caps speed; Opal absent on cheaper tiers.
