## Decision D8 (product/decisions.md)
- The gate measures fill over **in-scope rows**, not the whole category. A row is in scope when it has a maker source (purifiers: maker
  or ENERGY STAR), is not maker-marked discontinued, is not ruled out by a known value on the preset's `scope.by` filter ("NVMe",
  "Wi-Fi 7", "120 Hz panel", "has USB-C", "45 W+ port"), and the key is not `not_applicable` for it. Unknown always stays in, and a key
  is never scoped by its own value. Pass = 80% on every key **and** ≥2 brands with values. Each card states "Covers N brands (list) ·
  X of Y in scope", plus the exclusions and the brands not covered. The list opens on the in-scope rows, with "show all".
- Rejected: "sold in the last N years" (no dates: `gen.release_year` is 0% in monitors and routers; maker `discontinued` is the
  proxy). "Brands whose source has this key" (selects on the outcome and hides parser bugs). The full shown slice (shown already
  as the match / might-match split).

## Structural fixes (docs/presets.json; API file regenerated with scripts/presets.py; frontend-smoke + smoke.mjs pass)
- Purifier: filter price is preview-only (`claim/air-purifiers-structured@1`). Now **smoke CADR ≥ 260 cfm**, basis the AHAM rule
  (room area = 1.55 × CADR; 400 sq ft → 258). 191 of 206 values are ENERGY STAR-certified. **Passes now: 94.9%, 67 matches.**
- Router: `wifi.streams_6` is printed only by ASUS and Ubiquiti, and it is not carried in the index, so it always measured 0.
  TP-Link prints no per-band streams (0 of 106). New `router-wifi7-6ghz`: Wi-Fi 7 + 6 GHz band, ranked by the 5 GHz PHY rate,
  streams as a column.
- SSD: NAND type is rarely printed (TEAMGROUP 23 of 173), so the new `ssd-dram-2tb` is DRAM-only, with NAND as a column.
- Monitor: `usb.usbc_inputs ≥ 1` added as the scope filter. Its fill is 0% until port lists are parsed (the honest number).
  4K120 TV: a 120 Hz panel filter added. Power bank: scoped by its 45 W+ port.

## Key findings
- 2,855 of 4,234 monitors are listing-only rows (ENERGY STAR / DisplayHDR) and can never carry PWM or USB-C PD.
  637 of 1,371 TVs are discontinued LG models.
- Parser gaps, not sourcing gaps: LG and ASUS monitors have 0 `gen.size_class` although their pages print it (only `viewable_diag`
  is taken). Vizio "Backlight Type" sits in `unmapped`. Lenovo `mem.max` misses cluster in 8 newer PSREF families.
- Affirmative-only keys cap fill: `flk.pwm` is only ever `none`, `pd.pps` only ever true and `has_6ghz` only ever true. A "no" needs
  an A1-approved complete-enumeration rule. Power-bank PPS cannot pass without one: Belkin and mophie never print PPS.
