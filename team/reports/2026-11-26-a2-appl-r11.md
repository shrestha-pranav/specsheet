**A1 rulings as implemented:**
- Pattern detection: `*` `#`, a trailing R/L, or a trailing lowercase xxx or "(x = ...)" note. Patterns are keyed x_energystar_model_pattern, get a "-es-pattern" id and use their own extractor. Their UPCs become covered_upc aliases.
- A SKU links to a pattern only through additional_model_information or a unique whole-string match. Nothing merges or expands, and escover@1 transfers no claims.
- Conditions: test_procedure appendix_a / appendix_c1 is a basis_condition. Adjusted volume has its own key. better_than/percent_less columns use unit %.
- Refrigerant: the R-number only, with GWP only from an explicit token. Re-filings that disagree on a field leave it unknown. cert.energy_star is never false.
