## Mapping / data-quality notes
- Everything keeps `raw` literal; value proposed only when unambiguous (panel enum, 'Color Depth 8-bit (6-bit+FRC)' -> 6-bit+FRC).
- ES `maximum_luminance_candelas` -> hdr.sdr_typ (basis typ/min unstated); ES contrast values <100 (2, 8, 1) are dropped as
  non-ratios (105/266 rows had "2"). ES HDR/color/USB-C fields are yes/no or generic: kept in fingerprints.unmapped.
- Lenovo 'Refresh Rate' -> res.refresh_max (native vs OC unstated); response time left unmapped (MPRT/level mix needs a basis field).
- ES model_number is the certified string, not orderable; identity.md says match loosely as candidate only.
