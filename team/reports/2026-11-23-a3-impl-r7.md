## What changed
- **Bundles (scripts/bundles.py, transform bundles@2).** No SKU-digit inference. The number-only part_sig is replaced by canon_desc (full text,
  only plurals fold). Colours come from a reviewed list, not any slash part. Pipe tails stay as unparsed (contents incomplete); a list-like
  tail is unparseable. 'Free X', 'X (Free)' and 'X (Free Y)' are dated promotions, never contents. 0 quantities are rejected, and a capacity
  note must equal the page's own Wh. Minting needs the same exact multiset in every observation and one stated market (unstated is not US).
  Punctuation-variant SKUs, a plain option vs a bundle title, and unparseable observations go to identity_conflicts, not back to aliases.
  The evidence level is published in `bundle_evidence` (one listing = manufacturer_store_listing). Links carry parser
  `<adapter>@N+bundles@2`, and the transform fingerprint is in audit.parsers and current_fp, so editing bundles.py is parser drift.
- **TVs (new scripts/tvcert.py, transform tvcert@1; brand-generic, so Vizio is covered).** Wildcard records are never models and never merge.
  They give directed `covered_by_certification` edges (model -> energystar-tvs:<pd_id>), published as `certifications`. Matching is
  whole-string with the documented '*' (one alphanumeric) and escaped literals; any other wildcard is proposal-only. Guards: brand, US record
  + US model key, size, dated record, year; disagreeing overlapping records are quarantined. Separate allowlists: `pwr.energy_star`, or the
  4 power keys given applicability evidence. A crosswalk pair yields `same_model` (no merge) only with a human `by`, a quote naming both
  strings, an exact brand suffix, one market on pair, rule and both entities, no contradicting pair, and agreeing sizes. A failed alias audit
  demotes the alias to `proposed_same_hardware`.
