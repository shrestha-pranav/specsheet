## What changed (each exit criterion = a fixture in tests/test_review3.py, 23 tests)
1. **Trusted semantics.**
   - `_robovac` navigation, obstacle sensors and mop type trust only explicit mechanism phrases. Generic laser/camera/visual, negation, a single
     or uncounted rotating mop, trademarks and mixed mechanisms become an `Unresolved` claim: status unknown, literal in `raw`, reason in `quote`.
   - `basis_for` needs an affirmative, figure-specific attribution. The new enum value `mfr_reported_certified` covers a maker page that says
     "certified by". Negated text or a bare "laboratory" gives mfr_unstated.
   - csa_dcl@2 re-checks the exact name match on the stored ledger row.
   - `_powerbank.all_port_total`: a total needs a printed combination whose members name every individual port, or a labelled Total Output row.
     Rejected, with the reason kept: two-port '&' without a '+' context, subset totals, unprinted sums, mode-dependent totals, a single port.
   - Port segmentation now keeps whole names ("USB-C OUT+USB-A OUT1", "Wireless OUT (Phone)", "Total [..(..)..]").
2. **Identity.**
   - `_robovac.group` unions on equal names, or on a shared SKU only when the pack_base names match. A conflicting SKU is recorded, never
     bridged. `corroborate()` replays this rule on stored bundles at parse time; no member was quarantined in the current data.
   - `shopify_powerbanks.identity_check` and import.py: a contradictory page is deferred until every corroborated page is in. If its model already
     exists, the page is quarantined; if it is the only page, it is kept with its id. Either way it gets an `identity_conflicts` row (9 rows).
   - Quarantined: P41-E1 page, P73-E1 qi2 page (P76 SKUs), P61L-P1 palm-size page (PPT01), UGREEN 75982SR / 75701SR editions, UGREEN
     35524 / 35338 '+' bundles. Flagged but kept as the only page: iniu-p41 (E2 SKUs), iniu-p21 (E1 SKU).
3. **Conditions.**
   - Anker title capacity now goes to cap.class_mah, never to cell_mah.
   - cap.rated_mah carries basis conditions `rated_voltage_v` / `rated_current_a` (new in the power-bank profile).
   - Unlabelled dual Wh/mAh figures stay unresolved.
   - Profile labels: "All-ports simultaneous output" and "Best single-port output". Belkin was removed from the total and cell-mAh sources.
   - Robot dims/weight outside the bounds and Roborock robot+dock cells are now unknown claims with the literal and reason (6 Roborock cells, 1 iRobot cell).
