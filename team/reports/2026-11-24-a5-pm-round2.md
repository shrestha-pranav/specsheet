## Key findings
- **Biggest internal gap:** laptop `mem.soldered`, `mem.slots`, `disp.panel_type/nits_sdr/refresh` are 0% of 2,419 rows, though
  psref-mapping.yaml maps them and PSREF publishes them. This is an extraction or gate bug, P0 for A2. Monitor `flk.pwm` is 0% of 4,133.
  SSD HMB is 0%, NAND 6%, controller 5%. TV dimming zones <1%. Power-bank PPS ranges 0%. Router SoC 0%.
- **Demand evidence:** at least 7 PCPartPicker threads ask for SSD DRAM/NAND filters, and staff declined because makers don't document
  it and swap parts. LG and Samsung TV panel lottery is confirmed by FlatpanelsHD. TFTCentral retired its flicker DB. RAM-upgrade guides
  all say "look up the exact product number". Samsung 45 W PPS confusion: banks list only "PD 45W", and regional variants differ.
- **Agents:** OpenAI's own shopping research scores 52% product accuracy on multi-constraint queries. RTINGS paywalled its measurements
  in 2026, citing AI reuse. Existing MCP product servers are price/stock/retailer tools or Icecat marketing sheets. None answers
  PWM/DRAM/PPS/panel-per-size with provenance. Our slot is sourced, explicit-unknown answers.
- **Brand gaps:** laptops (no Dell/HP/Apple), SSDs (no Crucial/Kingston), TVs (no Sony; Hisense 5 rows), routers (no eero/Ubiquiti/GL.iNet),
  power banks (no Baseus/Xiaomi/Samsung pack), robovacs (no Shark/eufy), strips (no APC/Belkin).
- Caveats: Reddit did not surface via search (site: queries returned nothing), so the forum evidence is non-Reddit. Most claims rest on
  search snippets. Demand scores are judgment, not search volume.
