## Decisions (product/decisions.md D9, D10)
- **D9: a source ladder.** (a) The maker's other publications, then (b) certifier records and certificate-derived rules, then (c) crawl-permitted
  independent measurement, then (d) evidence-backed corrections from people. Each candidate source has a disposition proposal (D9 table, YAML in the
  A2 brief §1). New policy key `crawl: agent | human_only | none`: agents fetch only `agent` domains; people may cite `human_only` ones.
- **Two of the three "maker silences" are not silence:**
  - **Belkin prints PPS** in per-SKU support articles ("(BPB036) … 45W max USB-C fast charging (PPS supported)"), and robots allows them.
  - **Lenovo monitors:** 95 of the 105 unknown rows print a TÜV Eye Comfort cert. Certipedia's star table says 1-4 stars = "Flicker reduced" and
    5 stars = "Flicker free". 33 rows are 5-star, so a derived rule fills them. 3-star, 4-star and unstarred rows never map.
- **SSD DRAM really is silent**, and no structured source passes:
  - TechPowerUp SSD DB: still excluded (robots blocks Claude-User and anthropic-ai, and AI use is reserved).
  - ssdwiki: wrong (lists SN7100 as "DRAM: Yes" next to its own HMB note).
  - hardware-corner: unsourced. No Wikipedia table exists. Tom's Hardware/Future: the terms ban crawler extraction.
  - So SSD DRAM comes from person-cited family facts through the corrections flow, with D10's two-sample revision guard.
- **Notebookcheck moves from `free: false` to `measurement, free, crawl: agent`.** Robots is open, and the impressum allows ≤100-word quotes with a link and
  the name. It has small monitor yield but is the laptop-PWM source later.
- **ChargerLAB moves to `free, crawl: human_only`** (it blocks ClaudeBot and anthropic-ai). Excluded: RTINGS (paywall intent), TPU `/ssd-specs/`,
  ssdwiki, aggregators (lead-only).
- **Ruled out:** TCO gen 9/10 (no flicker criterion, PDFs read); Eyesafe 2.0 (blue light only); ENERGY STAR and EPREL (none of the 3 facts);
  USB-IF Integrators List (login). The public usb.org list is a probe only.
- **D9.2 corrections:** GitHub issue form with evidence types:
  - E1 label photo, E2 maker URL, E3 a D9 measurement URL, E4 your own measurement. Text alone is never evidence.
  - The sync writes `data/corrections/<issue>.yaml` (no handle; images hashed, never copied). A human writes `accept_claim`/`reject_claim` in the ledger.
  - The kind follows the evidence (label photo = `official`, measurement = `measured`). Abuse: ≤10 open per account, COI box, burst pause.
- **D10: presets count measured and derived values**, with:
  - a per-kind fill breakdown on the card;
  - "sources disagree" moving a row from match to might-match;
  - measured values never replacing maker values; provenance badges on every value.
  - Rejected: official-only gates (two presets could never pass) and a 25% cap.
