**Outcome: wired end to end. The match rate is close to zero by construction.** 1 of 974 target entities binds: 1 of 192 chargers, 0 of 197 power banks, 0 of 585 laptop models (and 0 of their configurations).

- **Why the rate is low:** USB-IF names the company that filed the listing, which is mostly an OEM (Luxshare 665 rows, CE Link, Channel Well, Best Buy China, Xentris).
  - No row is listed under Anker, Belkin, Apple, mophie, Satechi, AUKEY, Choetech, Baseus, INIU, CUKTECH, KUXIU, Sharge, Nitecore or EcoFlow.

  - **Identity quality is weak:** an OEM part number is not a retail SKU. My recommendation is no cables category from this source alone.

- **Identity rule (scripts/usbif.py, usbif-attach@1):** all of these must hold, otherwise nothing attaches:
  - the listing company is the brand (an explicit COMPANIES map);
  - a part-number token equals the entity's own mfr_model, mfr_sku or mtm (identity.norm_value); no prefix, stem or suffix matching;
  - the product type matches the category; otherwise it becomes a proposal (type_mismatch);
  - the row is not Obsolete and has a TID.
  - cert.usb_if is only ever true; absent means unknown. TID, date and product type are emitted only when exactly one TID binds.
