# A1+A2: televisions (new category `tvs`), 2026-11-23

## Decisions / open questions first
- **Result:** 914 model entities (894 published rows in docs/api/v1/tvs). LG 524, Samsung 240 (195 site + 45 ES-only), TCL 83, plus
  ES-only models from Sansui 29, Philips 12, XITRIX 6, Hisense 5, Loewe/Emerson/Clear Tunes 4 each, Sceptre/RCA/Insignia 1 each.
  The import accepted 23,740 claims and left 1,297 as candidates; 850 of the claims are status-unknown (unresolved) preview evidence.
  0 id conflicts, 0 parser drift. Profile: 58 specs in 11 groups, 3 condition dimensions. It validates against the schema.
- **Coordinator: approve `claim/tvs-structured@1`.** It is pending and was locked by this import. It is one policy with explicit
  extractors (4), source_ids (4) and per-source key lists (ES 12, Samsung 41, LG 44, TCL 47), known and normalised values only.
  Deliberately not trusted: refresh.max_hz, hdr.peak_nits, Samsung panel.backlight (it comes from a "Contrast" row) and ES panel.backlight
  (Mini-LED sets certify as "Direct-lit").
- **Identity: one size = one model number** (QN65S90FAFXZA, OLED65B5PUA, 65QM8K). "QN65S90F" and "S90F" are the family and carry no
  panel facts. Because of this, the panel lottery across sizes is a per-model fact by construction. A lottery within one model string
  would be a `hw_rev` revision, but no source exposes one.
- **Retailer-exclusive designations:** these are grouped only on the maker's ENERGY STAR statement ("Same as basic model except model
  designation"), which yields 71 aliases of kind `declared_same_hardware`, e.g. QN65S90DAF <- QN65S92DAF, QN65S90DDF. Everything else
  becomes a proposal: 90 `possible_match` links. Examples: LG OLED42C5PUA ~ OLED42C5AUA, and Samsung site QN..XZA ~ the ES certified string.
  Nothing was merged.
- **Open (A3):**
  - ES certifies LG only as wildcard families ("OLED65C4AU* is the same as OLED65C4PU*"). The importer skips these (63 rows), so LG's ES
    power data and its declared channel equivalences are unused. A pattern-to-model `declared_same_hardware` relation would fix this.
  - ES-only and site entities stay separate models (e.g. QN65S90FAF vs QN65S90FAFXZA) and are linked only by the proposal.

## Review-3 lessons applied (fixtures in tests/test_tvs.py, 17 tests)
- **No negation-blind enums.**
  - Dolby Vision, HDR10+, VRR, ALLM, HGiG and ATSC 3.0 come only from their own rows with an affirmative value. `false` only comes
    from a row whose value is just "No" (ATSC: a row naming 1.0 and not 3.0).
  - A format missing from a list stays unknown. Samsung has no Dolby Vision row at all.
  - Shifted Samsung rows are ignored: a FreeSync row holding accessibility text, a Product Type row holding the resolution.
  - "UHD Dimming" and "Precision Dimming" are not counted as local dimming.
- **Explicit membership (hdmi.ports_4k120).** Counted only from per-port statements: Samsung "4K 144Hz (for HDMI 1/2/3/4)", TCL "HDMI
  1-2: 4K@144Hz | 3-4: 4K@60Hz", LG "4ea (supports 4K 120Hz ... (2 port))". A generic LG feature list with no port count is unresolved.
- **Bases in conditions.**
  - Power carries power_basis: energy_star_on_mode, doe_federal_test, energy_star_standby, maker_typical or maker_unstated.
  - ES standby also carries standby_network. Peak brightness carries brightness_basis.
  - The ES certification limit is never stored as a product value.
- **Native vs marketing refresh.**
  - Motion Rate, TruMotion, Motion Xcelerator and Clear Motion Index go to refresh.marketing.
  - TCL "144Hz/DLG 288Hz" becomes native 144 plus dlg_hz 288.
  - Samsung "120Hz (Up to 144Hz)" becomes native 120 plus max 144 (max is preview only).
- **Plausibility quarantines rather than erases.**
  - About 30 Samsung sets print mm under an "inch" label; these and cells with no unit stay unresolved, keeping the literal.
  - 28 ES rows with swapped resolution become absences and are not re-ordered.
  - "2100+ zones" is unresolved. "Up to 4000" is kept with qualifier up_to.
- **Conflict-aware attribution.**
  - TCL spec boxes are attributed only by a Model row equal to the SKU, or by a unique size match that agrees with the SKU's size.
    14 of 83 SKUs could not be attributed, so they have no claims.
  - A printed size that contradicts the size in the model number is unresolved.
