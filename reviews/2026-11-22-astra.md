## Verdict

The product is worth building, but the plan currently promotes uncertain identity matches into authoritative specifications too easily. Ship a narrow, curated charger catalogue first: make identity and evidence prerequisites for publication, move image extraction into the core pipeline, and choose FastAPI + Postgres when a backend becomes necessary.

## Top risks ranked

| Rank | Risk | Concrete consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Incorrect identity links contaminate otherwise correct facts.** | A valid ChargerLAB measurement becomes a false US-SKU specification when the regional mapping is guessed. |
| 2 | **The extraction ladder stops before finding useful specifications.** | Valid JSON-LD supplies names and prices while PPS and power-sharing tables remain unread in images. |
| 3 | **The schema erases operating conditions.** | Peak, sustained, advertised, measured, single-port and simultaneous output become one misleading wattage. |
| 4 | **Source access and retention assumptions are too optimistic.** | Blocked retailers, incomplete feeds and API retention restrictions break both coverage and reproducibility. |
| 5 | **Claims and confidence appear rigorous without enforcing evidentiary rigor.** | A source URL and matching quotation cannot establish correct product, port, region or interpretation. |
| 6 | **Configurable products are represented as independent option sets.** | The catalogue implies nonexistent laptop configurations and incorrectly merges partially specified listings. |
| 7 | **Scope expands before acquisition economics are demonstrated.** | Nine brands, laptops, crowd reputation and paid-API infrastructure compete with fixing the core dataset. |

The pilot totals check out: **150 records, 69 with PPS=true, 77 unknown, 29 with ranges, and 63 low-confidence records**. Those are record counts, not yet verified SKU counts or verified specification counts.

## Section-by-section critique with concrete changes

### 1. Goal and scope

Define the initial product as **a source-backed comparison of known charger configurations in one market**, with explicit unknowns.

Resolve three scope contradictions:

- README’s “≤ $25” use case requires prices, while the plan excludes price tracking. Remove that filter until an explicitly separate offer service supplies timestamped prices.
- “GaN wall chargers” already includes desktop stations, power strips and products whose GaN status is unknown. Use `category=usb_charger`, with form factor and GaN as separate attributes, or enforce the narrower boundary.
- “One record per SKU” is not what the pilot contains: several records combine regions, colours, bundles and uncertain model aliases.

Choose a useful first acceptance query: **“Find US-plug chargers with a documented PPS profile supplying at least 3 A throughout a specified voltage interval.”** This forces correct per-port profiles, identity and provenance before adding broad catalogue coverage.

### 2. Data model

**Keep family/model/variant as navigation concepts; do not make them the entire identity model.**

| Concept | Recommended meaning |
|---|---|
| Family | Editorial grouping for browsing; not a source of automatically inherited electrical specifications. |
| Model | Manufacturer platform or hardware design, with evidence-backed common properties. |
| Configuration/variant | A specific physical configuration, independent of which seller lists it. |
| Trade item/package | A purchasable package: one configuration, a multipack or a bundle. Carries package-specific identifiers. |
| Listing | A source’s representation of something, possibly ambiguous or changing over time. |
| Revision | An evidenced hardware/firmware change with bounded or unknown applicability. |

A single-unit package can remain invisible in the UI. It still matters in storage: **a manufacturer SKU, physical configuration and purchasable package are not interchangeable concepts.**

Concrete pilot failures:

- Anker `A2665` and `A2146` share one record even though notes explicitly say their relationship is unresolved.
- Belkin `WCH001dqWH` and its cable bundle share one `model_numbers` array.
- INIU `A12-E1` occurs in both single and two-pack listings. That is a shared component model, not a duplicate package.
- Satechi’s US/EU/UK/AU codes share a record with `foldable_plug=true`, without establishing applicability to every region.

**Change inheritance rules.** “Store only deltas” is compact but dangerous when absence means unknown. Distinguish:

- Missing child assertion: inheritance may be allowed.
- Explicit child unknown or conflict: block inheritance.
- Not applicable: do not inherit.
- Parent claim: applies only to its documented scope.

Materialize complete resolved variant records for reads, including the ancestor claim and applicability rule. Do not infer that a field is invariant because only one variant has been observed.

### 3. Acquisition

#### 3.1 Source registry and trust

Replace the global trust ladder with **source authority by field, identity scope and evidence type**.

A regulatory source is authoritative about its filing or certification scope, not automatically about retail configuration, PPS capability or current availability. An FCC identifier can identify an installed radio module shared by unrelated host products; it cannot establish that two laptops have the same hardware. FCC rules explicitly provide for host labels referring to an enclosed module. [FCC modular-transmitter requirements](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2021-title47-vol1/pdf/CFR-2021-title47-vol1-sec15-212.pdf)

Similarly:

- A manufacturer manual spanning several models needs row-level applicability.
- A third-party review supports observations about its tested specimen.
- A retailer identifier can be useful while its description is wrong.
- A search snippet is evidence of what the search service displayed, not a captured manufacturer document.

Add registry fields for access method, locale, allowed use, retention, attribution, expected coverage, last successful fetch and maintenance owner.

#### 3.2 Discovery versus extraction

The separation is correct. The claim that one `products.json` request returns everything is not a reliable contract.

Implement discovery as a **checkpointed enumeration process**:

1. Enumerate available feeds, sitemap indexes and collection pages.
2. Follow pagination and record completion.
3. Capture source product IDs, variant IDs, URLs, locale and identifier assertions.
4. Reconcile additions and disappearances only after a successful complete enumeration.
5. Schedule extraction by expected value and missing fields.

A partial feed or blocked page must never produce mass “discontinued” updates.

Use search only for targeted gaps: an exact model plus “manual,” a missing identifier bridge, or a delisted product. Repeated broad agent searches are an expensive and weak discovery mechanism.

Discovery should create a **source observation**, not immediately create a canonical product. A page may represent many selectable variants, several bundle options or an entire product family.

#### 3.3 Replace the extraction ladder with field-directed extraction

“Stop at the first rung that yields valid data” is the wrong stopping condition. A valid record with a name and all-null electrical fields has accomplished almost nothing.

Use this sequence:

1. **Fetch and classify the document.** Detect HTML, PDF, image, consent page, challenge, truncated response and error page.
2. **Extract structured identity and asset links.** Parse JSON-LD, feeds, hydration data and selected variant state.
3. **Run deterministic adapters** over specification tables, manuals and relevant JSON.
4. **Read native PDF text before OCR.** Preserve page numbers, table headers and footnotes.
5. **OCR specification images early** for brands known to publish key fields there.
6. **Use an LLM for unresolved structure or interpretation**, with bounded evidence input.
7. **Render only when required to acquire missing content**, then feed the result back through the same extraction stages.

Browser rendering is an acquisition method, not inherently the final extraction rung. A rendered page may still require deterministic parsing or OCR.

Make stopping criteria explicit:

- Target fields found with sufficient evidence;
- Source exhausted;
- Identity remains ambiguous;
- Budget exhausted;
- Access prohibited or blocked.

Store the reason. “Not found” and “not attempted” must not look identical.

**OCR must be a first-class path.** For UGREEN and Baseus, the pilot demonstrates that it is central to the product’s value:

- Discover original-resolution assets from galleries, lazy-load attributes, manuals and CDN links.
- Deduplicate identical image bytes across listings before OCR.
- Preserve image hash, bounding box, table headers and adjacent footnotes.
- Check decimal points, voltage/current units and port labels.
- Route ambiguous critical cells to human review or a second extraction method.
- Preserve the original OCR text when proposing a correction.

For Anker’s `3..25A`, store the literal source and a proposed normalization. Do not silently turn it into verified `3.25 A`.

A matching quotation is necessary but insufficient: it can come from a comparison table’s wrong column or a different regional variant. Validation must include **subject, scope, units and operating conditions**.

Demand-driven extraction should prioritize a background queue. **Do not trigger paid browsing or OCR synchronously from arbitrary public queries**; that creates latency and a direct spending-abuse path.

#### 3.4 Change detection, cost and anti-bot reality

Use multiple fingerprints:

| Fingerprint | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Raw response hash | Exact capture identity and debugging |
| Relevant structured/specification hash | Detect meaningful specification changes |
| Asset manifest and image/PDF hashes | Detect changes invisible in page text |
| Identity/variant fingerprint | Detect changed SKUs, selectors and package contents |

A cleaned-text hash misses image-only changes. An unchanged HTML response does not prove unchanged independently hosted images. Periodically revalidate important assets.

Cache fetches separately from extraction. Extraction cache keys should include **content hash, schema version, adapter/prompt version, model version and relevant OCR configuration**. Store extraction outputs; replaying a nondeterministic model is not reproducibility.

Track cost per **accepted field** and **published configuration**, including review time. The reported 620k tokens are not an economic model without model prices, retries, successful coverage and human effort.

For v1, set fixed budgets for discovery requests, image bytes, browser jobs, LLM tokens and review minutes. Prioritize jobs by likely useful information gained, rather than source tier alone.

Anti-bot handling needs an explicit operating policy:

- Respect `Retry-After`; apply exponential backoff and a domain circuit breaker.
- Stop repetitive 403/503/challenge retries.
- Treat blocked fetches as unavailable evidence, not product disappearance.
- Prefer permitted feeds, manufacturer support documents or a documented manual acquisition.
- Do not make proxy rotation or CAPTCHA circumvention part of the business model.

A paid unblocker changes retrieval success, not permission or downstream reuse rights.

#### Legal/ToS: a concrete architectural constraint

**“Keep raw snapshots forever” must be removed as a universal rule.** Best Buy’s published API terms limit caching content to temporary storage of no more than 72 hours, directly conflicting with the proposed permanent archive. Do not assume an official API permits this catalogue’s intended storage and redistribution. [Best Buy API terms](https://developers.bestbuy.com/legal)

Use a source policy that controls:

- Whether automated access is permitted;
- Whether raw documents may be retained, and for how long;
- Whether content may be sent to an external extraction provider;
- Whether quotations or images may be republished;
- Whether derived data may be redistributed commercially.

Publish normalized facts and source links by default. Keep permitted raw captures private; do not commit manufacturer photos, full manuals or contributor evidence to the public repository by default.

US copyright generally does not protect facts, but may protect their expression; that distinction does not settle contractual restrictions or other jurisdictions’ database rights. Robots rules also are not access authorization. Obtain source-specific permission or legal review where the intended reuse is material and unclear. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-general.html), [RFC 9309](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html)

### 4. Entity resolution

**Move this before publishing extracted specifications, not into a later phase.**

Several current rules should be reversed:

- **Do not strip multipack, generation or regional information before matching.** Parse those into features; preserve the original title.
- **Do not normalize away regional suffixes.** A regional suffix may encode plug, keyboard, warranty, electrical design or package contents.
- **Do not treat FCC ID as a hardware-equivalence key.**
- **Do not treat CPU disagreement as a universal model veto.** CPU is explicitly a laptop variant axis elsewhere in the plan.
- **Do not treat missing specifications as agreement.**
- **Do not automatically create a new canonical entity for every low-score observation.** Keep an unresolved observation when information is insufficient.

Use relation-specific decisions:

`same_configuration`, `same_package`, `variant_of`, `member_of_family`, `contains`, `possible_match`, `different`, `insufficient_evidence`.

Only actual equivalence relations should be transitive. “Same family” and “contains the same charger” must never participate in a union-find merge of products.

**Identifiers need namespaces and applicability.**

Preserve raw values, leading zeros and meaningful punctuation. Store issuer, scheme, marketplace/region, package level, observed validity and evidence. Validate GTIN check digits, but remember that valid syntax does not prove a retailer attached it correctly. Packaging levels have their own GTINs. [GS1 packaging identification](https://resources.gs1ie.org/helpdesk/how-do-i-identify-my-products-and-cases)

A useful matching order is:

1. Find candidate packages/configurations using identifiers.
2. Check contradictory trusted identifiers, package composition and applicability.
3. Auto-link only documented unambiguous cases.
4. Use soft features to rank remaining candidates.
5. Send ambiguous decisions to a human.

Image similarity is a retrieval hint: brands reuse renders across wattages, colours and generations. Matching port counts and wattage is also insufficient to bridge regional codes.

**The pilot’s crosswalks must become explicit assertions.** UGREEN `65636 ↔ X774` and Baseus `E0122400 ↔ E01207` should remain proposed relationships until supported. Their test-derived claims must not inherit the certainty of the underlying measurement.

#### Configurable laptops

For “2026 Lenovo Yoga 7i,” the marketing name identifies a search group, not a configuration.

- Resolve the actual platform and manufacturer model identifiers from evidence. Treat the plan’s illustrative codes as placeholders.
- Store fixed MTM configurations exactly as documented.
- Include market, keyboard, OS, GPU, panel properties, battery and relevant connectivity alongside CPU/RAM/storage.
- Keep incomplete retailer configurations unresolved when multiple MTMs remain compatible.
- Distinguish factory configuration from retailer-installed RAM/storage upgrades.

A CPU/RAM/storage/panel tuple can narrow candidates; it does not necessarily uniquely identify a SKU.

**CTO option sets require constraints.** Store allowed combinations, exclusions, dependencies, market and effective dates. Do not take a Cartesian product of options or assume every observed CPU pairs with every panel.

Only mint an actual configuration when it is observed or explicitly documented as orderable. A hypothetical valid combination can have a configuration fingerprint without being labelled a purchasable SKU. Lenovo’s PSREF search itself notes that special-bid and CTO models are unsupported, so PSREF cannot be assumed to enumerate that universe. [Lenovo PSREF search](https://psrefstuff.lenovo.com/search/)

#### Revisions

Support revisions now in the schema, but create them only with evidence. A newer review showing different behavior does not establish a production revision.

Store revision label, affected model/configurations, serial or batch bounds when known, firmware and evidence. Unknown revision applicability must remain unknown; do not apply the newest measurement to every unit sold under the model code.

### 5. Claims, resolution and confidence

The claims approach is appropriate. The proposed precedence rule is not.

**Measured and advertised values usually answer different questions.** Satechi’s 60 W peak/45 W sustained behavior and its 67 W charger reportedly throttling after ten minutes illustrate this. A measurement should not overwrite the advertised rating.

Likewise, CUKTECH’s input-voltage-dependent output requires conditions. A scalar `max_total_w=110` cannot represent every market and operating mode.

Resolve only claims with equivalent:

- Subject and revision applicability;
- Field meaning;
- Port and active-port configuration;
- Input voltage and relevant conditions;
- Evidence type: declared, negotiated, measured or derived.

Then use source authority, specificity and evidence quality. Recency is relevant only within the same applicability scope.

Replace record-level confidence with separate assessments of **identity linkage, extraction accuracy and evidentiary support**. A correctly extracted review does not make an uncertain SKU crosswalk reliable.

Also:

- Source independence must be tracked. Three retailers copying one description are one evidentiary origin.
- Crowd confirmations are not independent merely because accounts differ.
- A missing value in a later extraction should not retract an older claim automatically.
- A resolver must be allowed to return `conflicted` without selecting a winner.

### 6. Crowd contributions

Start with **correction reports and maintainer review**, not reputation-weighted truth.

Require the contributor to identify the exact product/package, affected field and evidence. For measurements, request port, cable, active outputs, input voltage, firmware and test method.

Minimum moderation states: `submitted → needs_evidence → accepted/rejected → superseded`.

Acceptance should create or approve a claim or identity relationship; it should not directly edit the resolved value. Identity changes and cross-region mappings deserve at least as much review as “model-defining” specifications.

For evidence uploads: keep originals private, remove public EXIF, redact serial numbers/order details, enforce content and size limits, and establish contributor permission to store and display evidence. Fetch submitted URLs through an isolated fetcher that blocks private/internal destinations.

Email verification is an abuse barrier, not proof of expertise or independence. Defer reputation and automatic promotion.

### 7. API and API keys

Make API objects explicit: family, configuration and package are different resources, even if a convenience `/products` endpoint aggregates them.

Change the contract:

- Brand is required for model-number lookup; marketplace is required for retailer IDs.
- Lookup returns candidates, relationship type and match status when ambiguous.
- Filters have documented units, null semantics and an allowlisted grammar.
- Default filters use accepted values; unknown/conflicted values do not silently qualify.
- Use stable cursor pagination with deterministic sorting.
- Return `schema_version`, `dataset_version`, resolution status and freshness.
- Preserve merged IDs through canonical redirects; a split may require an ambiguity response rather than an arbitrary redirect.

**API keys from the first dynamic API are reasonable; keys for public static JSON are not enforceable.**

Minimum paid-ready model:

- An owner/account separate from its keys;
- Random high-entropy keys, stored as a digest, displayed once;
- Key prefix, scopes, expiration, rotation and revocation;
- Versioned plan entitlements attached to the owner;
- Per-key burst limits and per-account period quotas;
- Atomic quota enforcement and durable request usage records;
- Defined treatment of errors, cache hits and retries;
- `429` plus retry guidance.

Authenticate before serving protected cached responses. Do not embed a privileged key in the public UI. Do not call a public static mirror “key-protected.”

Later billing still requires payment state, entitlement changes, reconciliation and support. It is not literally a billing switch. Public bulk data also means the paid value must be convenience, freshness, limits or service guarantees—not presumed exclusivity.

### 8. UI

Default to one comparable hardware configuration per row; show packages and seller listings separately.

Expose:

- Declared versus measured specifications;
- Peak versus sustained output;
- Region, plug and revision applicability;
- Unknown, conflicted and unreviewed states;
- Evidence location and retrieval date;
- Why a comparison result qualifies.

For laptop matrices, show documented configurations and progressively selected options, not a giant Cartesian grid.

A discovery-only product should display “specifications not yet verified.” Its empty arrays must not imply zero ports or no supported protocols.

### 9. Architecture

**Choose FastAPI + Postgres for v1.**

The pipeline is already Python, and the workload centers on relational identity, evidence, review transactions and indexed filtering. Postgres JSONB fits claims; typed projections fit search. One Python codebase and one relational database are a better fit than introducing an edge application plus a separately operated extraction pipeline.

Workers + D1 is viable for a small, read-heavy catalogue with simple submissions. Its limitations are not immediate capacity blockers at 150 records. However, D1 has a 10 GB paid per-database limit, single-threaded execution per database instance, and usage accounting based on rows scanned/written. Those characteristics add constraints without materially simplifying the proposed Python ingestion and matching system. [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/), [D1 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/)

Recommended deployment:

- Static frontend and published snapshots;
- One FastAPI service;
- One Python worker using leased Postgres jobs;
- Postgres for metadata, claims, decisions and projections;
- Private object storage for permitted evidence;
- Backups and a tested restore procedure.

Avoid making free-tier availability the architecture criterion.

“Everything is re-derivable from blobs + claims” is incomplete. Preserve **human decisions, identity events, policy versions, configuration constraints and extraction outputs**. These are essential inputs to replay.

### 10. Phases

The ordering is wrong: the pipeline publishes data before identity is addressed, while API-key timing contradicts section 1.

Build identity primitives and package separation before broad extraction. Bring keys in with the dynamic API. Delay laptops until the charger workflow proves useful coverage and affordable review.

### 12. Pilot results

Treat the pilot as a **discovery and adversarial-fixture corpus**, not production-ready data.

Specific issues to turn into acceptance cases:

| Pilot evidence | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Five Baseus records still contain `"unknown"` model identifiers | Reject placeholder identifiers while retaining unresolved observations. |
| UGREEN `95536` stores PPS maximum 49.5 W although notes say the actual cap is 45 W | Separate advertised profile bounds from deliverable power; prevent the erroneous filter result. |
| Spigen `PE2106` has 65 W single-port output explicitly described as an assumption | Publish unknown or a labelled inference, not an accepted measurement/specification. |
| EcoFlow 65 W retractable model lists `45+15+15` in its sharing text | Flag the 75 W allocation against the stated 65 W envelope; retain the source assertion. |
| Baseus `CCGP150513` has conflicting 25+20 and 45+20 splits | Represent competing conditional claims, not a prose field. |
| INIU `I624` has conflicting port configurations | Hold identity/spec resolution rather than selecting whichever source was parsed first. |
| Anker `A2340/A2029` includes an explicitly unconfirmed prior | Remove the prior from accepted identifiers. |

These are not merely missing fields. They demonstrate why notes must be converted into machine-readable uncertainty, conditions and identity disputes.

### 13. Hosting: v0 to v1

GitHub Pages is suitable for a public project preview and downloadable dataset.

Call the v0 interface a **static dataset distribution**, not the dynamic API described in section 7. It cannot execute arbitrary filters, authenticate keys, meter users or accept submissions.

Publish:

- Immutable versioned releases;
- A latest-release manifest;
- Per-entity JSON and category indexes;
- Schema and dataset versions;
- Checksums, generation time and per-record freshness;
- ID alias/tombstone mappings.

Keep raw evidence and credentials out of the public repository. Preserve the schema contract across backend migration, but do not promise identical static and dynamic query behavior.

Plan a hosting move before commercial service launch: GitHub Pages explicitly restricts hosting online businesses and commercial SaaS, and has size/bandwidth limits. [GitHub Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)

### 14. Answers to the open questions

| Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Three levels or explicit revision? | Keep the three browsing levels, add package/listing distinctions, and support optional evidenced revisions now. Do not force every product through a fourth hierarchy level. |
| JSONB claims or typed category columns? | **Both:** append-only, schema-validated JSONB claims plus typed indexed resolved projections. Avoid querying an unrestricted field/value store for every public filter. |
| LLM adjudication? | Human decisions at this scale. An LLM may summarize evidence and rank candidates, but should not authorize ambiguous merges. |
| Manufacturer text versus facts; retailer APIs? | Publish normalized facts and links. Retain/reuse text and images according to source-specific rights. Official API access does not imply permanent storage or redistribution rights. |
| Minimum gold set? | The proposed 300 pairs/category is useful for development, insufficient to broadly trust auto-linking. Evaluate each rule and difficult identity stratum separately. |

For the last question, with **zero errors among 300 independently sampled accepted links**, the approximate one-sided 95% upper bound on error is still 1%. Demonstrating below 0.1% requires roughly 3,000 error-free accepted links under the same assumptions—not 3,000 mostly easy negative pairs.

At this scale, manually approve nontrivial links. Build a gold set emphasizing generation collisions, regional suffixes, multipacks, shared renders, missing identifiers, incomplete laptop configurations and revisions. Hold out entire families or generations to reduce leakage. Measure cluster contamination and review load alongside pair precision/recall.

## What to cut from v1

- Dell/HP ingestion and full laptop coverage.
- Automatic LLM merge adjudication.
- Reputation-weighted crowd voting.
- Paid retailer unblocking.
- Regulatory-database-wide discovery.
- Arbitrary user-triggered deep extraction.
- Price filters and price history.
- A general-purpose configuration constraint solver.
- Billing implementation.
- Global catalogue completeness.

**Do not cut:** OCR, per-field evidence, package separation, unresolved identity states, reversible decisions, conditional specifications or review tooling. Those are the product.

## Specific schema changes

### Replace the current schema document

`schema.json` is a descriptive template, not an enforceable JSON Schema. Replace it with versioned schemas using explicit types, required fields, enums, bounds and rejection of unexpected properties. Keep explanatory prose separately.

Add the following logical records:

| Record | Essential fields |
|---|---|
| `entity` | `id`, `kind`, `brand_id`, `category_id`, `lifecycle_state` |
| `relationship` | `from_id`, `relation`, `to_id`, `status`, `evidence_ids`, `decision_id` |
| `identifier_assertion` | `subject_id`, `scheme`, `issuer`, `raw_value`, `normalized_value`, `market`, `package_level`, `status`, `evidence_id` |
| `source_snapshot` | `source_id`, `url`, `locale`, `fetched_at`, `http_status`, `content_hash`, `asset_manifest`, `retention_policy_id` |
| `observation` | `snapshot_id`, source product/variant IDs, selector context, observed identifiers |
| `claim` | `observation_id`, `subject_id`, `field_path`, `value`, `unit`, `claim_kind`, `conditions`, `evidence_locator`, extraction version |
| `resolution` | Subject/field/conditions, selected claim IDs, conflicting claim IDs, status, policy version, dataset version |
| `revision` | Parent subject, label, batch/serial/firmware applicability, evidence |
| `package_component` | Package, component configuration or unresolved description, quantity, evidence |
| `identity_event` | Link/merge/split/rejection, affected IDs, actor, reason, evidence and rule version |

Claims need source time when available, capture time and extraction time separately. Define idempotency using observation, evidence location and extraction identity—not timestamps alone.

### Replace ambiguous charger fields

| Existing field | Replacement |
|---|---|
| `model_numbers[]` | Scoped identifier assertions; uncertain crosswalks are relationships. |
| `ports[]` | Stable output IDs, physical labels and `connector_form=socket/captive_cable`. |
| `max_total_w`, `max_single_port_w` | Conditional rated/peak/sustained/measured power claims. |
| `pd_fixed_pdos` | Per-output arrays of numeric voltage/current profiles with protocol classification. |
| `pps_ranges` | Per-output APDO records with voltage bounds, current limits, power-limit information and conditions. |
| `pps_max_w` | Derived value with formula, inputs, applicable limits and derivation version; null when not defensible. |
| `port_sharing` | Active-output scenarios and allocation/shared-group constraints. |
| `epr_avs` | Separate EPR support, SPR AVS and EPR AVS capabilities/profiles. |
| `dimensions_mm` | Numeric dimensions plus measurement scope: plug folded, extended, excluded, cable included. |
| `cable_included` | Package component; captive cable remains a hardware output. |
| `safety_certs[]` | Certification/compliance assertions with scheme, issuer, identifier, applicability and evidence. |
| `status` | Separate manufacturer lifecycle, source availability and last-seen timestamps. |
| `confidence` | Per-resolution evidence and identity assessments. |
| `field_sources` | Claim/evidence references supporting nested field paths and multiple sources. |

Do not equate EPR fixed-voltage support with evidence of an AVS profile. Likewise, multiplying a PPS range’s upper voltage by its current limit does not establish deliverable power; the USB source-power tests explicitly account for power constraints. [USB-IF source-power test specification](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB-C%20Source%20Power%20Test%20Specification%20V1.2%202026%2011%2004.pdf)

Finally, give absence explicit semantics: `unknown`, `not_applicable`, `conflicted` and `not_yet_extracted`. An empty list should mean **known to contain no entries**, not “the scraper did not find anything.”
