## Bundles: the current corroboration rule is insufficient

- Agreement is consistency among collected observations, not independent corroboration: one parsed observation can mint. State that evidence level explicitly, and require a manufacturer SKU-to-contents statement for acceptance. The report overstates “title names member”: digit containment can infer the base model from an add-on-only title. (`scripts/bundles.py:103`, `scripts/bundles.py:176`, `team/reports/2026-11-23-a3-impl-r6.md:9`)
- `part_sig` discards descriptive distinctions whenever numbers exist: “200W rigid solar panel” and “200W flexible solar panel” both become `200w`. Different contents can pass agreement, after which the shortest title supplies the published contents. Use exact component IDs or conservative full descriptions with reviewed synonym rules. (`scripts/bundles.py:112`, `scripts/bundles.py:191`, `scripts/bundles.py:209`)
- Title cleaning can erase evidence: arbitrary alphabetic slash parts count as colours, and every pipe tail is discarded. `Complete` fabricates an unitemised component; `<model> Complete` alone then satisfies the two-item threshold. Preserve unknown text and mark contents incomplete instead of treating these parses as explicit contents. (`scripts/bundles.py:31`, `scripts/bundles.py:44`, `scripts/bundles.py:95`)
- Freebies are handled inconsistently: `Free Panel` is dropped, but `Panel (Free)` loses its marker and remains a paid component. Record promotional items separately from stable contents, with observation dates; do not claim an exhaustive purchase manifest after dropping them. (`scripts/bundles.py:74`, `scripts/bundles.py:88`)
- Quantities accept zero; repeated equivalent text components are not aggregated. Reject nonpositive counts and canonicalize a multiset before agreement. Unparseable option identifiers are skipped, so the “every observation” guarantee depends on downstream alias collision handling; conflicting candidates are themselves restored as configuration aliases. Quarantine disputed identity instead. (`scripts/bundles.py:64`, `scripts/bundles.py:145`, `scripts/bundles.py:192`, `scripts/bundles.py:205`)
- Package grouping strips punctuation and omits market/time. Distinct regional SKUs or reused SKUs with changed contents need explicit market and effective-period scope, rather than permanent conflation or permanent rejection. (`scripts/bundles.py:173`, `scripts/bundles.py:218`)
- The relation policy pins adapter versions but names no version/digest of the shared code that actually decides membership. A change to `bundles.py` can change accepted relations under the same listed adapter rules unless the dependency is included in the audit lock. (`data/decisions/policies.yaml:1831`, `data/decisions/policies.yaml:1836`, `scripts/bundles.py:210`)
