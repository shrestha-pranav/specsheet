# Chargers: notes (A1+A2, 2026-11-24)

- What matters to a buyer and what the stores hide: the labelled total is the best combination, not all ports at once (Anker A2688: 100 W total,
  89 W with three ports); per-port PDOs/PPS ranges decide whether a Galaxy gets 45 W (PPS 5 A) or a laptop gets 20 V/5 A or 28 V EPR.
- Coverage is uneven: Anker prints full PDO tables; Belkin prints one 'Wattage' + port counts; UGREEN half its pages use an Amazon-attribute
  template ('Input Voltage: 240 volts', 'Wattage'); AUKEY, Plugable and Nekteck pages carry no spec rows at all (identity only, preview).
- Maker data errors are kept as printed, never "fixed": UGREEN Uno 65W (35856) lists USB-C1 '15W Max'; Belkin BBC032 title 65W vs Wattage 20W
  (left unresolved by the title check).
- PPS is rarely printed as a range (6 models); 'PPS' in a protocol row gives pd.pps only. EPR (28 V+) is printed by Anker A2345 and UGREEN 55549.
- Not covered: Apple/Samsung/Google first-party adapters (JS-rendered stores), Baseus (colour SKUs only), Lisen/ESR (Amazon ASINs as SKUs).
