## Dataset: ODbL 1.0

The compiled dataset (the files under `docs/api/` and any release, bulk or API export of them) is made
available under the Open Database License 1.0: <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/> (full text there).
In short: you may copy, adapt and use it, including commercially, if you attribute it, share adapted
databases under the same licence, and keep it open.

- **Individual facts are not claimed.** A wattage, a port count or a model number is a fact. The licence covers
  the database as a whole (selection, arrangement, structure, and the identity and review work), not each value
  taken on its own.
- **US federal government sources are public domain.** Values taken from U.S. federal works (for example the
  U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR product data) carry no restriction; we still credit them. Other sources are credited
  per value and keep whatever rights their owners hold in their own prose, images and marks. We republish
  specification values with source links, not marketing text or images.
- **Trademarks** belong to their owners. Brand and certification names appear only as factual identifiers.

### Attribution for a produced work

When you publish something produced from the dataset (a chart, a table, an app, a model output), include:

> Data: specsheet (ODbL), sources linked per value

The brand name in that line follows the site (`docs/site.json`). Link to the site when you can.
